What it is

Israf Guard tackles food waste in Malaysia. Restaurants offer surplus meals at lower prices, and students find affordable food nearby instead of it going to waste.

Who it serves

Students looking for affordable meals, and local restaurants that want to reduce food waste.

What we showcase

  • Affordable meals
  • Reducing food waste
  • Supporting local restaurants
  • A cleaner Malaysia
