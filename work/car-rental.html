What it is

An hourly car rental platform. The screens on this page are an interface concept; the full case study and live link will be published together with the project.

Who it serves

Customers who need a car for a few hours rather than a full day, and rental businesses that want to offer hourly bookings online.

What we showcase

  • Browsing vehicles by type
  • Choosing a number of hours and a time range
  • A clear reservation step on desktop and mobile
